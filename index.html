11 Friday the dinner 9/11 · 25 years
19:30dinner

COTE — Butcher's Feast unticked

Michelin-starred Korean steakhouse in Flatiron. Four cuts, banchan, stew, soft serve — $82 a head, about $430 all-in for four with tax and twenty percent. That lands inside the $400–600 you set and still leaves room for drinks after.

It's a table where the food keeps arriving and nobody has to perform being impressed. With two couples, one of whom you're hosting, that beats a silent tasting menu.

One hard catch: COTE has no vegetarian version. If Lucia or Lucas doesn't eat meat, this pick dies — go Semma instead (Michelin star, South Indian, deeply vegetarian, ≈$550–600 all-in for four). Different Resy window: 15 days out at 9:00am, so Thu Aug 27.
22:00free

The Tribute in Light

Friday is the 25th anniversary of September 11th. Eighty-eight searchlights throw two beams into the sky from dusk until dawn, visible for sixty miles.

Everyone's instinct is to go downtown. Don't — the 25th will be crowded and heavily policed, and the beams read better from a distance anyway. Your side of the river is the good seat. The Newport waterfront looks straight at them across the water, six minutes from your door, and it costs nothing.

It will be the most memorable thing on this itinerary, and it isn't on any list.

12 Saturday closing act
12:30the two of you

Centurion New York unticked

55th floor of One Vanderbilt. The dining room, The Gallery, is Daniel Boulud's. Members-only club, open 11am–10pm — and since Saturday lunch is just you and Lucas, this is the right slot for it. Two people, no logistics, and it's the flex without needing to be a $600 dinner. Budget ~$150 for two.

Verify access before you promise it. Amex's own terms list Centurion New York as Centurion card access; Platinum is described only as "certain times," which is not something to discover at the elevator. There's a public dining path — call 212-597-9070 between 10am and noon on a weekday and book The Gallery outright. Do that this week, not in September.

Worth knowing: the Centurion Lounge already on your list at a 9 is the airport one. This is a different room, and it's unticked. Also — a 5am Sunday flight means the airport lounge won't even be open, so this is the only version that happens.

Six things I can't answer for you

Each of these changes something above.

  • Is Lucas or Lucia vegetarian? This is the only fork between COTE and Semma, and it's due before 10am tomorrow.
  • Which airport, both directions? A 5am from EWR is a 2:15 car; a 5am from JFK is closer to 2:00, and from LGA you get twenty minutes back.
  • Where are they sleeping? If it's your place, the Wednesday landing is easy and Saturday's 2am exit is easy. If it's a hotel, both ends get harder and the Saturday plan should end nearer to them.
  • Who's awake at 2am Sunday? Decide before Saturday night starts, because it determines who's drinking at Campbell.
  • Does Sneha's place actually work for a Thursday BBQ — space, grill, the dog underfoot with two guests? If it's tight, Ainsworth's patio is the swap.
  • Is Saturday too full? Lunch, sunset, a bar, a show, then an airport run. Cutting BATSU! is the pressure valve, and it's the one thing here you can cancel cheaply.
