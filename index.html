SEPTEMBER 11 · 25 YEARS

The one thing on this itinerary you don't have to book

Friday is the twenty-fifth anniversary. If the Tribute in Light runs as it has every year, eighty-eight searchlights throw two beams up from Lower Manhattan from dusk until dawn, visible for miles.

Everyone's instinct is to go downtown for it. Don't. The 25th will be crowded and heavily policed, and the beams read better from across the water anyway — the Newport waterfront looks straight at them, six minutes from the front door, and it costs nothing. Whatever else Friday becomes, end it there.

    L&L

    Statue City Cruises is the only authorised ferry, out of Battery Park. Anyone selling you a "Statue tour" on the street is selling you a harbour cruise that doesn't land. Book online in advance — pedestal tickets go weeks ahead, the crown is months.

    Backup, and an honestly good one: the Staten Island Ferry is free, runs around the clock from Whitehall Terminal, and sails right past her. Same photograph, no ticket, no line. If Battery Park is a mess on the 25th, take this and buy yourselves an afternoon back.
